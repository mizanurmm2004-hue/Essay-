About This Project

Problems We Observed

1. Finding Numbers: Elderly or uneducated parents can't type or search names on a smartphone. Saving a contact is too complex for them.

2. Emergency Failures: A parent calls their child 3–4 times in a medical crisis. The child is busy and misses every call. The parent is left helpless.

3. Out of Balance: When mobile credit runs out, elderly people get completely disconnected from family with no way to inform anyone.

Our Smart Solutions

1. Remote Contact Management: The parent doesn't need to do a thing. Children log in from anywhere in the world and remotely save numbers, photos, and voice tags on the parent's phone.

2. Smart Emergency Routing: If a parent calls a contact 3+ times without an answer, the system auto-detects the emergency and redirects to a trusted backup number.

3. Voice Label System: Tapping a photo plays a real recorded voice tag. Zero reading required.

4. Low-Balance Auto-SMS: When credit runs out, the app sends an automatic SMS to the guardian. Local emergency numbers are pre-installed based on location.

Global Vision

Callesy is designed for Bangladesh, Malaysia, India, and every developing nation where millions of elderly, uneducated, or digitally excluded family members deserve to stay connected — simply, safely, and with dignity.
