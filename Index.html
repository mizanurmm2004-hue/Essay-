Project Background & Goals

🔴 The Problems We Observed

1. Saving & Finding Numbers: Elderly or uneducated people cannot type or search names in a smartphone. They cannot even save a contact by themselves because technology is too complex for them.

2. Emergency Failures: When a parent calls their son 3-4 times during a medical emergency, the son might be busy at work and miss the call. The parent gets helpless and doesn't know who to call next.

3. Out of Balance: When the phone mobile balance finishes, old people get completely disconnected from their family with no way to inform them.

🟢 Our Smart Solutions

1. Remote Contact Management: The elderly person doesn't need to do anything. Their children can securely log in from their own phones (even from abroad) and remotely save numbers, add photos, and set up voice tags on the parent's phone.

2. Multi-Level Smart Emergency Routing: If a parent calls a primary contact (like their son) 3 or 4 times and it is not answered, our system auto-detects the emergency. It automatically diverts the next call attempt to a backup number, like a trusted neighbor or nearby relative.

3. Voice Label System: When the user taps a photo, it plays the real recorded voice tag (e.g., "This is your doctor"). No reading required! Children can also use full voice control to trigger video calls or messages.

4. Low Balance Auto-SMS & Pre-installs: If the phone runs out of money, the app triggers an optional offline automatic text message to the guardian. Local emergency numbers (Police, Ambulance) are pre-installed based on location.
